Andy's favourites

The culture he carries: clubs, bands, books, heroes and the phrases he returns to, assembled from twenty-five years of his own recorded words.

Every entry cites the records behind it. Entries marked reported rest on interviews or press accounts rather than words in his own voice in the archive. Portfolio mentions are excluded: a Culture Secretary praising cricket, or a mayor partnering with Coronation Street, is doing the job, not confessing a love. Where the archive is silent on a known enthusiasm, the gap is stated.

Football

Everton

The oldest allegiance on the record. Four months into Parliament he declared "20 years' experience of following Everton football club, home and away" source, and months later: "My family has poured tens of thousands of pounds into Everton football club over the years" source. As mayor he fought Everton's corner against the Premier League's points deduction, calling the process an "abuse of process" source, and as a leadership candidate posted "Football belongs to the fans" source. At Cambridge his tutor remembered him as "a footie lad" who often wore an Everton shirt source. All Everton records.

Rugby league

Leigh Centurions, and the game itself

His maiden speech honoured the town's other code: "Leigh Centurions rugby league club has a proud history" source, and he was pressing rugby league's case in health debates within months source. He served as President of the Rugby Football League, and restated the commitment as a leadership candidate: "Rugby league deserves more. As a former President of the RFL, I'll be a staunch advocate for the game in whatever office I hold" source. All rugby league records.

Music

The Smiths

The band of his life's turning points: he courted his future wife at Cambridge by talking about the Smiths source, gave them two of his eight discs in the 2010 leadership questionnaire (How Soon Is Now; There Is a Light That Never Goes Out) source, and was still duetting to them on Instagram in the week he became leader source.

Oasis

A documented tension. In 2010 he pointedly "managed to leave the Oasis CDs in the box" source; by the 2026 campaign, Definitely Maybe survived his on-camera favourite-albums ranking source, and as mayor he claimed the band's homecoming gigs for the city's growth story source.

The 2010 desert island list

The fullest single statement of his taste, given to Labour Uncut during the 2010 leadership campaign: the Smiths twice, the Pogues twice (The Sick Bed of Cuchulainn; Dirty Old Town), Stone Roses (Ten Storey Love Song), Morrissey solo (Everyday Is Like Sunday), Elbow (The Bones of You) and Massive Attack (Protection) source. Elbow reappeared in his 2026 blind-ranking reel source. Even the sceptical write-up conceded: "nor could any spin doctor choose three songs out of eight by Morrissey and two by the Pogues".

Books and authors

The Damned United, by David Peace

His book choice in the 2010 questionnaire source: Peace's novel of Brian Clough's 44 days at Leeds, a northern football tragedy in fiction.

Middlemarch, and reading English reported

He has named Middlemarch his favourite novel and, in the 2015 contest, The Complete Works of Shakespeare his favourite book source. The his-voice foundation is on the record: "I was lucky to read English. I think it's helped me in the political world to have confidence over communication" source.

Poetry

Tony Harrison, Larkin, Chaucer reported

Harrison's poetry, taught for his Cambridge interview, changed his degree from Spanish to English source; he has cited Harrison's V, written during the miners' strike, and Philip Larkin as influences on his politics, and named "20th-century poetry and Chaucer" as his literary loves source. A marked gap: none of this yet appears in his own voice in the archive, which holds no broadcast or long-interview layer where poetry talk lives.

Film and television

Cathy Come Home, as a weapon

Ken Loach's 1966 film is the reference he reaches for on housing: launching his renters' package as mayor, he called the moment "the 'Cathy Come Home' moment for this generation" source.

Christopher Nolan, in passing

The premiership's social operation cut his first-weeks montage as a mock trailer captioned "As Nolan intended", playing on the director's 35mm and IMAX evangelism source. Gaps stated: his known regard for Jimmy McGovern's and Ken Loach's wider work is not yet evidenced in the archive in his own words.

Political heroes

Nye Bevan

The most-invoked figure in his political vocabulary, across two decades. Defending against foundation hospitals in 2003: "At the creation of the NHS, Bevan trumpeted as one of its chief benefits..." source; answering "whether Aneurin Bevan would recognise today's NHS" in 2007 source; and as Health Secretary: "If Beveridge and Bevan were designing the welfare state today, I feel certain they would..." source. All Bevan records in his voice.

Beveridge, and the sixth giant

The National Care Service white paper was framed as completing Beveridge's welfare state, adding a sixth giant to his five evils: fear of old age source — a frame he has carried from 2009 source to the social care commitment of his premiership's first fortnight source.

Bishop James Jones

Not a hero claimed but a debt repeatedly acknowledged: the bishop's Hillsborough review gave him the phrase that organises his politics of accountability (below), and his Hillsborough record returns to the families' twenty-seven-year example throughout briefing.

The pub

Talisker, and the local

His 2010 luxury item was Talisker whisky source. As prime minister the affection turned into policy theatre: announcing the hospitality business-rates cut as "this round's on us" source, followed by the pub video with the Defence Secretary: "pubs deserve our backing" source.

Phrases he returns to

"The patronising disposition of unaccountable power"

Bishop James Jones's 2017 title source, adopted as his standing description of how the British state treats the wronged; eight records carry it. See them all.

"Two different worlds"

His summary of returning to Cambridge after Hillsborough: "it really brought home to me that the country was in fact two different worlds" source — the class geography that underpins the devolution argument.

"London-centric"

The workhorse adjective of his case against Whitehall, seventeen records from his final Commons months ("a problem in our political system: London-centricity" source) through his sworn Covid Inquiry evidence source to the mayoralty's closing interviews. See them all.
